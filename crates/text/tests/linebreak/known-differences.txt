# Measured cases where swb's line breaks differ from Chromium's. The test
# crates/text/tests/linebreak.rs prints the key of each difference; every key
# here must still differ. The comment above each group gives the reason.

# Chromium breaks Thai and Lao text at word boundaries (dictionary); swb has no
# dictionary and breaks SA text only at spaces.
cases word-break=keep-all hyphens=manual | 0E01 × 0E32 × 0E23 × 0E1A × 0E49 × 0E32 × 0E19 × 0020 ÷ 0E20 × 0E32 × 0E29 × 0E32 ÷ 0E44 × 0E17 × 0E22
cases word-break=keep-all hyphens=manual | 0E9E × 0EB2 × 0EAA × 0EB2 ÷ 0EA5 × 0EB2 × 0EA7
cases word-break=normal hyphens=manual | 0E01 × 0E32 × 0E23 × 0E1A × 0E49 × 0E32 × 0E19 × 0020 ÷ 0E20 × 0E32 × 0E29 × 0E32 ÷ 0E44 × 0E17 × 0E22
cases word-break=normal hyphens=manual | 0E9E × 0EB2 × 0EAA × 0EB2 ÷ 0EA5 × 0EB2 × 0EA7

# break-all: Chromium does not break where the shaping of combining marks
# continues across the position (two marks, an enclosing mark before a Thai
# mark, a Khmer subscript consonant).

# Control characters (they do not occur in real text): with break-all, a C0
# control after an ideograph does not break before a letter or `(` in
# Chromium; after a space and two C0 controls, Chromium does not break before
# U+00B4 (the pair table, measured after `a`, breaks there).
